Digital gauge

Volvo 740 / 940 · main instrument cluster

A round digital display for the main instrument cluster, the panel with the speedometer and rev counter. It reads live engine data wirelessly from an ECUMASTER ECU and shows it as a full service overview or one big reading per screen.

  • DATAWireless, live from an ECUMASTER ECU
  • STATUSWorking and proven. Every screen on this page is the real gauge.
  • FITTINGDIY, with a detailed step-by-step installation guide
  • PLACEMain instrument cluster, Volvo 740 and 940

A separate part from the centre frame. It goes in the main cluster, not in the frame’s 52 mm pods.

Ask about the gauge

Live data from your ECUMASTER ECU, wirelessly

Fig. B2 · data link

The gauge takes its readings straight from the ECUMASTER engine management unit in your car. The data travels wirelessly from the ECU to the gauge in the instrument cluster.

  1. 01 · SourceECUMASTER ECUThe engine management unit already running your engine provides the readings.
  2. 02 · LinkWirelessLive engine data reaches the gauge wirelessly.
  3. 03 · DisplayDigital gaugeIn the main instrument cluster, with the speedometer and rev counter.
